---
title: Results history
description: Your last 10 Culprit Finder results, kept on your own site.
---

![The Results tab with four past results](../../../../assets/screenshots/screenshot-5.png)

The **Results** tab keeps your last 10 results, newest first, on your own site only. The tab shows how many there are.

Each row shows the date, a result badge, the plugins involved, and how many answers it took, plus **Download** and **Delete**. Click the plugin names to open the full result with its report.

- **Delete** removes one result.
- **Clear all** removes every result.
- Undoing the answer that produced a result removes that result too.
- When an 11th result arrives, the oldest one is dropped.
- Uninstalling Culprit Finder deletes the history.

Nothing in the history is sent anywhere.
