Culprit Finder finds the plugin that causes a problem on your site, or the two plugins that conflict with each other. It switches plugins off **for your browser only**, asks after each change whether the problem is still there, and narrows things down. Your visitors and other administrators see the normal site the whole time, and nothing is deactivated.

## At a glance

- About 7 answers for 30 plugins: each answer halves the list of suspects.
- Catches two plugins that only fail together.
- Keep plugins on that the problem needs, such as your shop plugin for a checkout problem.
- Answer from the Culprit Finder page, the toolbar on any page, or the dashboard widget.
- Always a way out: Exit, an emergency exit link that works logged out, and an automatic end after 60 minutes without an answer.
- A support report with no site address, user names or emails, and your last 10 results kept on your site.
